1. Introduction

Lufio is a mobile application that helps you improve your well-being through guided breathing exercises. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run and improve the app, and we explain all of it below.

Lufio does not require an account. We never ask for your name, email address or password, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your practice history, check-in answers (how you feel after a session), favorites, custom exercises, reminders and settings are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Analytics

To understand which features are used and to improve the app, Lufio uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects anonymous usage events, for example:

  • sessions started, completed or stopped, with the exercise and its duration;
  • check-in answers after a session;
  • settings changes, reminders created or removed, visual styles selected;
  • paywall views and plan selections;
  • the breathing pattern of custom exercises (not their names).

Firebase also collects technical information: a random app instance identifier, device model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate country. Lufio does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps or websites.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data → Analytics. Google processes this data on our behalf and retains it for a limited period (no more than 14 months). Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Lufio Plus is offered as an auto-renewable subscription or a one-time purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

To check whether you have access to Lufio Plus and to restore purchases, Lufio uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase and subscription history (product, dates, store country and status) and basic device information. We also use it to check whether you first installed Lufio before it became paid, so that features which were free for you stay free. Learn more in RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

5. Apple Health

If you enable Apple Health sync, Lufio writes your completed sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. Lufio does not read any data from Apple Health. Health data is never shared with third parties, never sent to analytics and never used for advertising. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app.

6. Camera, Photos and Motion

When you share your statistics, you can choose a background from your photo library or take a photo with the camera. The image is used only on your device to create the picture you share. Lufio does not upload it anywhere. Some visual effects react to how you tilt your device. Motion data is processed on the device and is not stored or sent anywhere.

7. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not use push notification servers.

8. Contacting Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in the message. We use it only to answer you.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the European Union), you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to its processing. We process analytics data based on our legitimate interest in improving the app, and purchase data to provide the service you paid for. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for details that help us find your data. Contact us at the address below.

10. Children’s Privacy

Lufio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

12. Contact
